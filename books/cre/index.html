Errata

The list below summarizes known mistakes and typos in the texbook. Newer versions have been corrected to eliminate noted mistakes in older versions (i.e., the errata of Version 1.0 does not apply to Version 1.1, but the reverse is not true).

To submit new comments please use this Google Form: Feedback Form.

Version 1.1 Errata

  • Pg. 48, The Equilibrium Reactor (Ch. 2): The numerical value of \(K_1\) is 7.477e11, not 2.477e11. The calculated value of \(K_a\) is correct. (thanks Jessica!)
  • Pg. 85, The Continuously Stirred Tank Reactor (Ch. 4): The proper name is the Continuous Stirred Tank Reactor, not Continuously. Although the reactor is indeed stirred continuously, the word "continuous" is to distinguish the reactor model from the "batch" alternative.
  • Pg. 264, Special Cases of the PBR (Ch. 10): In the solution for Example 10.3 the problem statement incorrectly specifies that the liquid's viscosity should be 1e-5 Pa-s; it should be 1e-3. Separately, the Reynolds number should be 12730, not 1273 (the corresponding \(f\) and \(f_p\) values are correct).

Version 1.0 Errata

  • Pg. 57, The Equilibrium Reactor (Ch. 2): The summation term for \(\Delta H_{T_R}^{\circ}\) is missing the stoichiometric coefficient; it should be \(\sum\limits_{i=\text{A}}^\text{Z} \nu_i \Delta H_{f,i}^{\circ}\). (thanks Jared!)
  • Pg. 114, The Continuously Stirred Tank Reactor (Ch. 4): The numerator in the calculation of \(L\) should be 0.021, not 0.0.021. (thanks Anthony!)
  • Pg. 296, Multireactor Systems (Ch. 11): "It can also be useful to rearrange Equation (11.12)..."
  • Pg. 345, Experimental Determination of Rate Laws (Ch. 13): Lines 5 and 6 of the code should both be plot(t, ...) and not plot(log(t), ...) or plot(1./t, ...).
